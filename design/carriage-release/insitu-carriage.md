# Insitu-style carriage and release

Prototype carriage / gripper follows the ScanEagle SuperWedge release idea in Insitu U.S. Patent 7,128,294 (2006).

## Idea

The patent separates the **acceleration run** from a **brake at the end of the stroke**. Gripper arms travel with the carriage and meet that brake with it. When the carriage stops, the arms keep forward momentum and swing clear of the fuselage (patent Figs. 6A–6B).

For this launcher:

1. Arms hold the aircraft (football stand-in on the prototype) through the rail stroke.
2. A brake stops the carriage at the end of travel.
3. Arm inertia opens the grippers and clears the airframe; stop loads go into the carriage structure.

Spring rates, arm angles, and pad loads are ours to size. Geometry and clearances for the Fighter / football fixture are checked in validation test B.

## Prototype

Bed is strut + trolleys with an Insitu-style gripper and a football stand-in. That run checks hold / release timing and clearances. Final carriage bearings and rail material are separate decisions.
