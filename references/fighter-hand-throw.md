## Useful conversions

These are arithmetic on the table above, not extra manufacturer data.

- Wing area 72.5 dm² = 0.725 m².
- At the takeoff-weight limit, wing loading is under \(7 / 0.725 \approx 9.7\,\mathrm{kg/m^2}\).
- If both limits apply at once, airframe plus everything except the 1.5 kg payload is under 5.5 kg.

## Not on the page

Hand-throw speed, stall speed, cruise speed, empty mass, motor, and any acceleration or g limit. The proposal’s 15 m/s and 4.6 g rail example stay assumed until a manual or a measurement supplies them. An 11 kg airframe is above the stated takeoff limit.
