The deck is a platform. The sheet says the console and benches can be placed where the owner prefers. It does not give a clear rectangle for a rail, so the boat mount in the full-scale budget is still not costed. Inside length 5.71 m is long enough for a 2.5 m rail only if the console is not in that run.

Deadrise is the hull’s V angle. It is not the pitch angle in the wave table.

The pitch model uses 7 m. The sheet’s overall length is 6.90 m, and 7 m is inside the stated ±3% on dimensions. Recomputing the three assumed seas at 6.90 m changes the pitch amplitudes by less than 0.1°. The table is unchanged. Using the hull length 5.97 m instead of the overall length would cut the 1 s chop pitch from about ±4.1° to about ±2.5°, because that wave is shorter than the boat. The table keeps the overall length.
